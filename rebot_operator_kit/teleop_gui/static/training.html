KEEP

MARK FAILED

Choose one reason, then re-record. The two videos and Rerun timeline remain available for failure analysis but are excluded from training.

AUTO-RETURN

The follower returns to the pose captured when collection connected. Put the passive leader back in its captured pose, then scatter the can to a new random reachable position; reset frames are not stored.

Session home: place both arms in the exact home pose before Start collection. Their current poses are captured when the process connects. Exact operating sequence
Get 10 clean episodes into the conversion/training pipeline first, then continue the same fixed-camera dataset through 50, 100, and 150.

        
    
          
  1. Stop manual teleop.The collector needs exclusive access to both serial ports. The preflight must say Free.
    2. 
          
  2. Lock the two training cameras.Logitech overhead sees the whole task path; the Innomaker claw camera sees the gripper contact. Mac webcam index 3 remains excluded.
    3. 
          
  3. Run the camera check.Inspect both new snapshots yourself. Do not accept a technically bright image with a bad crop, clutter, or occlusion.
    4. 
          
  4. Stage one can.For the first 150 clean episodes, keep exactly one can visible; pick it once and place it in the fixed taped sorting zone. Keep cameras, mounts, taped zone, and lighting fixed.
    5. 
          
  5. Capture session home and start.Put both arms in the exact start pose, then Start collection. Keep them still until the connection captures that pose; afterward move the teleop arm smoothly and naturally.
    6. 
          
  6. Judge every take immediately.Finish & keep only one smooth, clean success. Delete jerky-but-successful takes too; for any bad take, choose a failure reason and use Mark failed & re-record.
    7. 
          
  7. Reset to a new can position.The ReBot follower returns home automatically. Manually return the passive leader, then scatter the can to a new random reachable position across corners, edges, and center; the next recording waits for both arms and the reset timer.
    8. 
          
  8. Review and validate.Replay any archived take in Rerun. Finish, keep & end session saves the good take before disconnecting. Stop & discard archives the partial take as aborted; validation must PASS all 7D follower-coordinate checks.
    9. 
          
  9. Hit the Stage-1 milestones.Hand off the first 10 episodes for a throwaway train immediately; keep collecting and checkpoint at 50, 100, and 150 clean episodes. Only after 150 add random extra cans in frame while still picking exactly one.
    10. 
          
  10. Train only the approved route.Use native 7D MolmoAct2, or get explicit approval for a 6D fixed-wrist-yaw adapter before using Newt.
