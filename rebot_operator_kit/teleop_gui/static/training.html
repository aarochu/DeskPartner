Exact operating sequence
Get 10 clean episodes into the conversion/training pipeline first, then continue the same fixed-camera dataset through 50, 100, and 150.

        
    
          
  1. Stop manual teleop.The collector needs exclusive access to both serial ports. The preflight must say Free.
    2. 
          
  2. Lock the two training cameras.Logitech overhead sees the whole task path; the Innomaker claw camera sees the gripper contact. Mac webcam index 3 remains excluded.
    3. 
          
  3. Run the camera check.Inspect both new snapshots yourself. Do not accept a technically bright image with a bad crop, clutter, or occlusion.
    4. 
          
  4. Stage one can.For the first 150 clean episodes, keep exactly one can visible; pick it once and place it in the fixed taped sorting zone. Keep cameras, mounts, taped zone, and lighting fixed.
    5. 
          
  5. Start one run.Put both arms in your chosen start pose and click Play / start one run. Move the teleop arm smoothly and naturally.
    6. 
          
  6. Judge and end.Click Save successful run only for one smooth, clean success. For a bad take, choose a failure reason and click Discard failed run. Either choice ends the process.
    7. 
          
  7. Reset manually.Return both arms yourself, scatter the can to a new reachable position, and click Play again. No return, timer, or next recording starts automatically.
    8. 
          
  8. Review and validate.Every saved episode is durable before disconnect. Every discarded or faulted take stays in the raw archive and is excluded. Validation must PASS all 7D follower-coordinate checks.
    9. 
          
  9. Hit the Stage-1 milestones.Hand off the first 10 episodes for a throwaway train immediately; keep collecting and checkpoint at 50, 100, and 150 clean episodes. Only after 150 add random extra cans in frame while still picking exactly one.
    10. 
          
  10. Train only the approved route.Use native 7D MolmoAct2, or get explicit approval for a 6D fixed-wrist-yaw adapter before using Newt.
